# The ICANS/CRS ratio tracks costimulation within CD19 and does not hold in BCMA

**Abstract**

**Background.** T-cell-engaging therapies cause cytokine release syndrome (CRS), a  
systemic myeloid response, and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome  
(ICANS). They co-occur in most patients and dissociate in many, but whether their ratio is  
a construct or a patient property has not been tested.

**Methods.** We analyzed 21,937 case-level reports carrying either phenotype term in the  
US FDA Adverse Event Reporting System, 20,732 from 2019 onward when the ICANS term  
existed, covering 14 products with ≥20 phenotype reports as primary suspect. The  
pre-specified endpoint was R = ICANS/(ICANS + CRS), a ratio of report margins that  
cancels the market denominator. CD28- and 4-1BB-costimulated products were compared over all  
21 label permutations, under nine analytic specifications and nine reporting strata, and the  
ratio was rebuilt in EudraVigilance. Two gene modules were localized in human brain single-nucleus  
data against 10,000 expression-stratified nulls, tested for inducibility in TNF-α-stimulated  
brain endothelium, and put through a network-proximity test whose minimum detectable effect  
came from inversion.

**Results.** R was 0.361 for CD28, 0.274 for 4-1BB and 0.222 for bispecific products  
(Δ = 0.087): both CD28 products ranked above all five 4-1BB products, the most extreme  
ordering a two-versus-five design can score; the ordering reproduced in EudraVigilance  
(Δ = 0.141; ρ = 0.92). The direction held in all eight testable strata and in seven of eight  
years, but separation failed in the tightest homogeneous slice (United States,  
physician-reported: Δ = +0.073, p = 0.20); restricting to single-phenotype reports  
attenuated Δ to 0.058 and restricting to high-acuity reports widened it to 0.247, while the  
ordering failed within BCMA. The modules localized in opposite directions across brain cell  
types (Δ = +6.03, z = +8.57) yet were both induced in brain endothelium. Network proximity  
gave 1.34-fold against a minimum detectable 11.9-fold, and the same null resolved 43
externally curated pairs only above tenfold.

**Conclusions.** Within CD19 the ratio is ordered by costimulatory architecture, but  
its support is confined to the modern phenotype, it fails within BCMA, and it matures with  
reporting experience. At 6,500 reports, ratios between 0.309 and 0.331 cannot be resolved at  
all.

**Keywords.** ICANS; cytokine release syndrome; chimeric antigen receptor T cells; bispecific  
T-cell engagers; pharmacovigilance; network medicine; neurovascular unit.



**标题**：ICANS/CRS 比值在 CD19 产品中随共刺激结构而定，在 BCMA 中不成立

**摘要**

**背景。** T 细胞接合类疗法会引起细胞因子释放综合征（CRS，一种全身性髓系反应）与  
免疫效应细胞相关神经毒性综合征（ICANS）。两者在多数患者中同时出现，也在不少患者中  
分离；但两者之间的这一比值究竟属于治疗构建体的属性，还是患者的属性，此前从未被检验。

**方法。** 我们分析了 FAERS 中携带任一表型术语的 21,937 份逐例报告——其中 20,732 份  
来自 2019 年及以后（ICANS 术语启用的年份）——覆盖 14 个产品，每个产品至少 20 份表型  
报告且以该产品为首要可疑药物。预设终点为 R = ICANS/(ICANS + CRS)，这是一个报告边际  
之比，消掉了被市场主导的分母。对 CD28 与 4-1BB 共刺激产品，我们枚举了全部 21 种分组  
置换，在九种分析设定与九种报告分层下比较，并在 EudraVigilance 中重建这一比值。我们在  
人脑单核转录组数据中，以 10,000 个按表达分层的零模型为对照，定位两个基因模块，并检验  
它们在 TNF-α 刺激的脑内皮中是否可被诱导；此外让一项网络邻近性检验通过反解得到其最小  
可检测效应。

**结果。** R 在 CD28 产品为 0.361、4-1BB 产品为 0.274、双特异性产品为 0.222  
（Δ = 0.087）：两个 CD28 产品均排在全部五个 4-1BB 产品之上，这是 2 对 5 设计所能评分的  
最极端排序；该排序在 EudraVigilance 中复现（Δ = 0.141；ρ = 0.92）。方向在全部八个可  
检验分层与八年中的七年成立，但在最紧的同质切片（美国、医生报告：Δ = +0.073，p = 0.20）  
上完全分离失效；只保留单一表型时 Δ 降至 0.058，只保留高危表型时 Δ 升至 0.247，而该排序  
在 BCMA 中不成立。两个模块在脑细胞类型间定位方向相反（Δ = +6.03，z = +8.57），却都在  
脑内皮中被诱导。网络邻近性给出 1.34 倍，而最小可检测效应为 11.9 倍；同一零模型把 43 对经外部注释确定的相关基因集只从十倍以上分辨出来。

**结论。** 在 CD19 产品内部，这一比值按共刺激结构排列；但其统计支持仅限现代表型，  
在 BCMA 中不成立，并随报告经验而成熟。在 6,500 份报告的规模下，0.309 至 0.331 之间的  
比值差异完全无法分辨。

**关键词**：ICANS；细胞因子释放综合征；嵌合抗原受体 T 细胞；双特异性 T 细胞接合器；  
药物警戒；网络医学；神经血管单元。
